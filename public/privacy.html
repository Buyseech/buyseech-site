Who is responsible for your data

BUYSEECH d.o.o. (details above) is the controller of the personal data collected through buyseech.com. For any privacy question or request, email igor@buyseech.com.

What we collect and why

The contact form

When you send the form, we receive your name, email address and website address. We use them only to look at your site, reply to you and, if you want to work with us, to prepare an offer. We do not add you to a newsletter and we do not sell or share your details for marketing.

Legal basis: taking steps at your request before a possible contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site, for example which pages and sections are viewed, how people arrive (search, links, social media), roughly where they are (country and city level), what device and browser they use, and whether the contact form was sent. Google Analytics uses cookies (such as _ga) to recognise returning browsers. It does not give us your name or email address.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in understanding and improving how the website performs (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time (see “Your rights”), block cookies in your browser, or install Google’s opt-out add-on.

Hosting and security

When you visit the site, our hosting and security provider automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. This is needed to deliver the site and protect it against abuse (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Email

If you email us directly or reply to our messages, we keep that correspondence to handle your request.

Who processes data for us

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: website hosting, content delivery and security.
  • Web3Forms: receives the contact form and forwards it to our inbox.
  • Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC: Google Analytics 4.
  • WPX Hosting: our email hosting.

Some of these providers process data outside the EU/EEA, mainly in the United States. Where that happens, transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Form enquiries and emails: as long as needed to handle your enquiry. If no business relationship follows, we delete them within 24 months. If you become a client, we keep correspondence for as long as the law requires (for example, for accounting).
  • Google Analytics data: stored for 14 months, then deleted automatically.

Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To use any of these rights, email igor@buyseech.com. We reply within one month.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Slovenian data protection authority: Informacijski pooblaščenec (Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia), Dunajska cesta 22, 1000 Ljubljana, www.ip-rs.si.

Links to other sites

Our site links to YouTube, LinkedIn and other websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you visit them.

Changes

We may update this page when our website or services change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
